Hanoi, Vietnam

The rooftop view probably gives it away. Hanoi is quite chaotic, but definitely worth a visit.

Shirakawa-go, Japan

This picturesque village was one of the highlights of my trip to Japan. Shirakawa-go is a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

Broken Bay, Nusa Penida, Indonesia

Broken Beach on Nusa Penida, seen from above. Along with Kelingking Beach, it's one of the must-see spots on this island just off the coast of Bali.

Tanah Lot, Bali, Indonesia

No filter needed! The stunning Tanah Lot Temple in Bali, surrounded by water at high tide.

Römer, Frankfurt, Germany

The Römer is Frankfurt's city hall and, of course, one of its main attractions.

Dallas, USA

Visiting Dallas? Don't miss the fantastic skyline views from Reunion Tower.

Matterhorn, Zermatt, Switzerland

It took me 30 years, but I finally got to see this iconic mountain with my own eyes. This photo was taken during a hike from Zermatt to Gornergrat.

Bangkok, Thailand

Bangkok by night. Nuff said.

Arc de Triomphe, Paris, France

Even Paris's famous Arc de Triomphe has an observation deck. Unfortunately, I didn't know that when I visited.

St. Petersburg, Russia

A Russian Orthodox church, instantly recognisable by its characteristic onion domes. Welcome to St. Petersburg!

Chain Bridge, Budapest, Hungary

The Chain Bridge connects the two sides of the city, Buda and Pest, leaving no doubt about where this photo was taken.

Helsinki, Finland

What a fantastic design! An apartment building in downtown Helsinki.

Golden Gate Bridge, San Francisco, USA

The iconic Golden Gate Bridge is still one of the world's longest suspension bridges – and the second-longest in the United States.

Yosemite Valley, California, USA

Yosemite National Park, California. More than just an Apple wallpaper.

Cloud Gate, Chicago, USA

Chicago's Millennium Park is home to Cloud Gate, better known as «The Bean».

The Great Sphinx of Giza, Egypt

The Great Sphinx of Giza, with the Pyramid of Khafre in the background.

Seoul, South Korea

Seoul's grand palaces offer a glimpse into Korea's royal past.

The Blue Grotto, Malta

A spectacular natural sea cave framed by dramatic limestone cliffs and crystal-clear Mediterranean waters.

Valletta, Malta

Colourful traditional Maltese balconies bring character and charm to the city's historic streets.

Toronto, Canada

The city's iconic skyline fades into the warm hues of a summer sunset, with the CN Tower rising above the horizon.
